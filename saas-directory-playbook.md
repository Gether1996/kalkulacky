- Thousands of tools exist globally

**Solution:** Curated directory of tools that work in Slovakia

**Problem 2: Slovak-Specific Needs**
- Accounting compliance (Slovak tax laws, VAT reporting)
- Invoice requirements (legal format)
- Data residency (some businesses need EU hosting)
- Slovak language support
- Local payment gateways (TatraPay, CardPay, etc.)

**Solution:** Filter/tags for Slovak-friendly features

**Problem 3: Decision Fatigue**
- Too many options in every category
- Analysis paralysis
- No trusted recommendations

**Solution:** Expert curation, comparison, clear recommendations

**Examples:**
- "shopify recenzia" (Shopify review)

- "shopify alternatívy" (Shopify alternatives)

## Risk Management

### Risk 1: Affiliate Programs Don't Convert Well

**Likelihood:** Medium

**Mitigation:**
- Test different tools, track which convert best
- Feature high-converting tools more prominently
- Improve conversion funnel (CTAs, trust signals)
- Consider lead generation model as alternative

**Impact:** Lower revenue but not fatal

### Risk 2: SEO Takes Longer Than Expected

**Likelihood:** Medium

**Mitigation:**
- Plan for 9-12 month timeline (not 6)
- Diversify traffic (social, email, partnerships)
- Focus on ultra-long-tail first (quick wins)
- Invest in link building

**Impact:** Delayed revenue

### Risk 3: New Competitor Emerges

**Likelihood:** Low-Medium (if you succeed, others notice)

**Mitigation:**
- Move fast, build content moat
- Build brand recognition
- Focus on quality (hard to replicate)
- Engage community

**Impact:** Competition, need to differentiate

### Risk 4: Affiliate Program Terms Change

**Likelihood:** Low (but happens)

**Mitigation:**
- Diversify revenue streams (not dependent on one program)
- Build owned audience (email list)
- Add direct monetization (featured listings)

**Impact:** Revenue fluctuation
