# Part 1 — Market overview (SEO-business lens)

## 1.1 The structural picture

| Country | Population | Avg gross wage/mo | Search engine reality | Language reach |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Slovakia** | 5.42M (2024) | €1,524 (2024) | Google ~96%+ [EST] | Slovak (≈5.4M); mutually intelligible w/ Czech |
| **Czechia** | 10.88M (2025) | ~€2,155 (Q4 2025) | Google dominant, **Seznam.cz still ~10–20%** [EST] | Czech (≈10.7M) |
| **Poland** | 36.5M (2025) | ~€2,114 (Mar 2025) | Google ~98% [EST] | Polish (≈38M) — biggest V4 prize |
| **Hungary** | 9.64M (2024) | ~€1,791 (Apr 2025) | Google ~97% [EST] | Hungarian (≈10M) — isolated language |

*Source for population/GDP/wage: Wikipedia Economy/Demographics pages, 2024–2026 figures.* [FACT]

**Key implication:** the combined V4 addressable population is ~62M, but you operate in **four separate language SERPs**. A piece of content ranking in Slovak does not rank in Polish. This is the central strategic tension — and your opportunity: localization is a moat, not a checkbox.

## 1.2 Language / localization advantages

- **Slovak ↔ Czech** are close enough that one operator can credibly run both with light adaptation, but Google treats them as distinct markets and users strongly prefer their own language. Czech is ~2x the Slovak audience for nearly identical effort. [FACT/EST]
- **Hungarian is linguistically isolated** — almost no spillover competition from German/English/Polish content. This means *weaker* competition inside Hungarian SERPs but also *no* content reuse; everything must be produced natively. Highest localization moat, highest production cost. [PROXY]
- **Polish** is the largest single market and the most competitive of the four; treat it as "scale later," not "start here."
- English/German products frequently have **no localized SK/CZ equivalent** in boring verticals (calculators, directories, comparison). This gap is the spine of this whole report.

## 1.3 Monetization potential (CPC / affiliate / lead-gen / ads)

- **Display ads (AdSense/Ezoic/Mediavine-tier):** SK/CZ RPMs are low — **[EST] €1–6 per 1,000 pageviews** for general content, higher (**€5–15 [EST]**) for finance/insurance/energy. Display alone is a thin living in these markets; volume must be large.
- **Affiliate:** real and underused. Networks that actually work in SK/CZ/V4: **Dognet** (V4-wide), **eHUB.cz/.sk**, **VIVnetworks** (CJ in CEE), **AffilBox**-powered programs, **Heureka** affiliate, plus Booking/Amazon DE for travel/goods. [FACT] Affiliate commissions in energy, finance, telco, e-shops are meaningful.
- **Lead generation** is the strongest model for a technical/SEO operator who wants to avoid client work: capture a qualified intent (solar quote, mortgage, care home enquiry, tradesperson) and **sell the lead** to providers. Lead values in SK/CZ are lower than DE/UK but real: **[EST] €3–€40 per qualified lead** depending on vertical (energy/solar/finance highest). This is semi-passive once the funnel + buyer relationships exist.
- **Paid directory listings / featured placements:** classic boring-money model for fragmented offline categories (care homes, tradespeople, vets, funeral services). **[EST] €5–€50/mo per listing.**
- **Micro-SaaS / subscription:** strongest in B2B (tender alerts, grant monitoring, compliance). Willingness-to-pay exists where the tool saves billable time or finds money. **[EST] €10–€100/mo B2B.**
- **Digital products:** legal templates, spreadsheets, paid reports — low-maintenance, fully passive once made.

## 1.4 Competition level vs. English-speaking markets

[PROXY, but high-confidence] SK/CZ SERPs for boring/commercial queries are **noticeably weaker** than EN/DE equivalents:

- Many top results are **outdated** (designed pre-2018, poor mobile, no schema, thin content).
- **Government and incumbent portals** rank but offer terrible UX — a well-built explainer/calculator routinely outranks or out-converts them.
- **Directories are spammy or stale** (scraped Firmy.cz/Azet-style listings, dead phone numbers).
- Domain authority bars are low: niche SK SERPs can often be entered with **dozens, not thousands, of quality pages and modest links.** [EST]

The flip side: **lower ceilings.** You win SERPs more easily but each win is worth less per visitor than in English.

## 1.5 Cross-border localization opportunities

The repeatable play: **build once in SK or CZ, prove the model, then clone the template into the other three languages.** Calculators and directories are the most clonable (logic + data structure travel; only labels, legal parameters and listings change). Pure content sites are the least clonable (full re-write per language).

Best cross-border angles (detailed in Part 7): tax/salary calculators (every country has the same need with different parameters), energy/solar subsidy tools, pension/benefits calculators, EV cost tools, cross-border worker (Pendler) tax tools.

## 1.6 Key risks

1. **Small ceilings:** a SK-only site may cap at low-to-mid hundreds of €/month; plan for multi-market or multi-niche from the start.
2. **Seznam.cz** in Czechia is a second SEO surface you must not ignore (Zboží.cz, Firmy.cz, Seznam search).
3. **Regulatory niches** (finance, health, legal) carry compliance exposure — stay on the informational/calculator/directory/lead-gen side, never give regulated advice.
4. **Data freshness debt:** calculators and grant trackers must be updated when laws change (annual tax tweaks, subsidy round openings). This is the maintenance tax on "passive" income here.
5. **Platform dependence:** Google algorithm shifts; AI Overviews compressing informational clicks — bias toward **transactional/lead-gen and tool intent**, which AI summaries cannibalize less.
6. **Lead-buyer concentration:** if 2–3 installers buy all your leads, they hold pricing power. Diversify the demand side.

## #1 — Solar / Photovoltaic & Heat-Pump Subsidy Calculator + Installer Lead-Gen

**1. Name:** *Energy subsidy navigator + solar/heat-pump calculator with installer lead-gen.*

**2. Countries/languages first:** Slovakia (Slovak), then Czechia (Czech). Both have active, well-funded state schemes. PL/HU as phase 3.

**3. Why now:** The 2022–24 energy shock permanently raised interest in self-generation. SK runs *Zelená domácnostiam* (SIEA, EU-cohesion funded) for PV/heat pumps/solar thermal; CZ runs *Nová zelená úsporám* (SFŽP). [FACT] Subsidy rounds open/close unpredictably, rules change yearly, and homeowners are confused about eligibility and real payback. That confusion is the product.

**4. Evidence of demand [PROXY]:** Persistent autocomplete for *"dotácia na fotovoltiku 2025"*, *"zelená domácnostiam kalkulačka"*, *"fotovoltika návratnosť"*; CZ *"dotace na fotovoltaiku"*, *"nová zelená úsporám fotovoltaika"*. Energy is among the highest-CPC verticals in CEE. Strong "People Also Ask" around payback, battery sizing, eligibility. Active Facebook groups for PV owners in both countries.

**5. Evidence of weak/fragmented supply [PROXY]:** Government portals are accurate but bureaucratic and non-interactive. Ranking content is mostly **installer blogs** (biased, thin) and a few media explainers that go stale within a year. No dominant neutral calculator that combines *eligibility + grant amount + payback + "get 3 quotes."*

**6. Main keywords & intents:**

| Intent | Slovak | Czech |
|---|---|---|
| Subsidy/eligibility | dotácia na fotovoltiku 2025, zelená domácnostiam podmienky | dotace na fotovoltaiku 2025, nová zelená úsporám podmínky |
| Calculator | kalkulačka fotovoltiky, návratnosť fotovoltiky | kalkulačka fotovoltaiky, návratnost fotovoltaiky |
| Cost | fotovoltika cena, cena tepelného čerpadla | fotovoltaika cena, cena tepelného čerpadla |
| Provider | fotovoltika montáž firma, recenzie | montáž fotovoltaiky firma, recenze |

**7. Example keyword clusters:** (a) *Grant rules* — eligibility, amounts, deadlines, how to apply, documents. (b) *Economics* — payback calculator, ROI, battery worth it, self-consumption. (c) *Technical* — panel sizing, kWp for a house, on-grid vs hybrid, heat-pump pairing. (d) *Buying* — price ranges, how to choose installer, contract red flags, reviews.

**8. Competitors & weaknesses:** Installer company sites (biased, only-our-product); SIEA/SFŽP gov portals (no calculators, poor UX); general energy media (stale, no lead capture). **None own the full eligibility→payback→quote journey.**

**9. Recommended format:** Content+tools hub: interactive eligibility wizard + payback calculator → "get up to 3 vetted quotes" form → leads sold/distributed to installers. WordPress + custom calculator (JS) is enough.

**10. MVP (2–6 weeks):** Slovak only. 1 payback calculator, 1 eligibility wizard for the current *Zelená domácnostiam* round, 8–12 cornerstone articles (rules, amounts, payback, battery, heat-pump pairing), and a simple quote-request form emailing 2–3 partner installers. Manual lead handoff at first.

**11. Advanced (6–12 mo):** Czech clone; installer dashboard + automated lead distribution/billing; per-region installer directory with reviews; price-index pages ("PV prices in SK Q-by-Q"); email capture + subsidy-round-opening alerts; heat-pump sizing tool.

**12. Monetization:** Lead-gen (primary, sell qualified quote requests); featured/paid installer listings; affiliate (battery, wallbox, monitoring); display ads on info pages; sponsored content from manufacturers.

## #2 — Mortgage & Refinance Calculator + Broker Comparison/Lead-Gen

**1. Name:** *Mortgage calculator & broker-comparison lead-gen portal (hypotéka SK / hypotéka CZ).*

# Part 6 — Validation plan (do this before building fully)

The whole point: **convert the [EST] numbers above into measured numbers cheaply, before sinking weeks of dev.** Budget ~€100–€300 and 2–3 weekends per idea to validate.

**1. Validate keyword demand.**
- Get real volumes: Google **Keyword Planner** (free with any Ads account), **Search Console** on any existing site, and a low-cost tool month (**Mangools/KWFinder**, **Ahrefs/Semrush** trial, or **Marketing Miner** which is CZ/SK-focused). Pull volumes for SK and CZ separately.
- Free proxies: **Google autocomplete + "People Also Ask" + related searches**, **Google Trends** (relative interest, seasonality, SK vs CZ), **AnswerThePublic/AlsoAsked**. Count how many distinct question-queries exist.
- Threshold to continue: a primary cluster with **≥ a few thousand combined monthly searches [EST target]** and a long tail of 30+ supporting queries.

**2. Validate monetization (most important, do early).**
- Lead-gen: **email 5–10 potential lead buyers** (installers, brokers, facilities) and ask plainly: "Would you pay for qualified enquiries, and how much per lead?" Get a number or a soft yes before building. This single step kills or confirms most ideas.
- Affiliate: confirm programs exist and check payouts — **Dognet, eHUB, VIVnetworks**, Heureka, plus vertical programs (accounting SaaS, insurers). Sign up; read terms.
- Ads: estimate RPM from comparable SK/CZ content; assume the **low end (€1–6)** until proven.

**3. Test SEO difficulty.**
- Manually inspect the SERP for 10 head + 20 long-tail terms: How old/strong are the top results? Check domain rating and referring domains of the top 5 (Ahrefs/Moz free tools). Look for **outdated pages, thin content, no calculators, weak mobile** — these are green lights.
- Score each idea's SERP as Easy/Medium/Hard. Prioritize where you see government PDFs + installer blogs ranking (beatable) over where strong comparators dominate.

**4. Test user interest (fake-door, ethically).**
- Build a **single landing page** describing the tool/service with a real CTA ("Get my payback estimate," "Compare offers," "Get 3 quotes"). On submit, show an honest message: *"Thanks — we're launching shortly, we'll email you."* No deception about what they'll receive; capture email with consent. This measures real intent, not just clicks.
- Drive cheap traffic: **€50–€150 Google/Meta Ads** to the exact keywords, or post in relevant FB groups/forums. Measure click-through and form-submit (intent) rates.

**5. Check affiliate/lead-gen partners (supply side).**
- For directories/lead-gen, confirm **buyers exist and are reachable**: count providers on Firmy.cz/Azet/Zlaté stránky in the category; call/email a handful. If providers won't pay or aren't contactable, downgrade the idea.

**6. Estimate revenue before investing.**
- Combine measured inputs: `monthly revenue ≈ sessions × CTR-to-action × conversion × value-per-action`. Use **your validation CTRs + the real lead price/affiliate payout you confirmed**, not my estimates. Build a tiny spreadsheet with conservative/realistic/optimistic columns.

**7. Metrics that say CONTINUE:**
- Confirmed lead buyer(s) with a concrete price, or confirmed affiliate payout that math-checks at realistic traffic.
- Beatable SERP (top results weak/old) on a cluster with real volume.
- Fake-door page converting **≥ ~5–10% of targeted visitors to email/intent [EST benchmark]**.
- A clear path to 30+ quality pages and your first links.

**8. Metrics that say KILL (or park):**
- No buyer will name a lead price and no viable affiliate program → no monetization path.
- SERP dominated by strong, fresh, well-funded incumbents across both head and long-tail.
- Fake-door CTR/intent near zero despite on-target traffic.
- Volume too thin to ever reach the traffic in the Part 4 tables at plausible RPM/lead value.
- Demand exists only in a single subsidy round with no evergreen fallback.

**Sequencing tip:** validate **monetization (#2) and SERP difficulty (#3) first** — they're the cheapest kills. Only build the fake-door (#4) for ideas that pass both.
